# Sketch
Long ago, a legendary Pokemon fell from space. It had an attunement to emotions, and was extremely susceptible to them, like the Sha. From this being came three legendaries, based on rage, rapture, and terror. Their presence inspire these emotions, which empower them, which inspire them, into a terrible loop.

When tensions grow between people in the region, the prime legendary awakens, causing the trio to awaken, causing tensions to overflow into war. Pokemon in general also grow more aggressive and leave their wilds to attack humans.

The legendaries are:
- Prime "Emotion parent": Psychic/Ghost
- Rage: Fire/Ghost
- Rapture: Poison/Ghost
- Terror: Ice/Ghost

Tensions are high, and the Prime is soon to awaken.

# Background
In the Kosis (psychosis) region, catastrophic war is well known, and emotion is repressed — such repression does work in keeping the prime legendary asleep. Pokemon battling is thought to prevent emotions from bubbling over, but it is slightly taboo.

A civil war breaks out soon after the game begins. It is fought between Separatists—people who believe the Pokemon League (whatever it’s called) is too close to government, and Unionists—people who believe the government should be the Pokemon League. The conflict has been secretly instigated by agents of Team Conjure so that they can control the prime legendary.

Gym leaders are military leaders. Each has four trainers. This mirrors the final Elite Four and Champion fights; they’re just another squad. Since battling is slightly taboo, gyms never took significant hold before they were outlawed as they were thought to instigate the last conflict (and were used as forts in the conflict).

# Story
The player is a seventeen-year-old named Luke or Trish. Since battling is slightly taboo in Kosis, they have not received a Pokemon of their own yet, nor have they befriended one since they traditionally stay away. The unchosen gender is the player’s fraternal twin sibling. Their father is a veteran of the last conflict (which was not strong enough to awaken the Prime), and their mother runs a store.

While engaging in some sort of festival, an entourage comes in, and 17+ year old children are going to be conscripted in preparation of a hot war (which has not yet begun) on behalf of Unionists. The festival ends early, and the kids are taken (only Luke and Trish here). Before they are taken, their parents quickly give them a starter of their choice, so they have a powerful starter to use instead of the standard-issue: Sprigatito (Grass/Dark), Litten (Fire/Dark), or Froakie (Water/Dark). They are heirlooms from a previous conflict. The player’s sibling takes the type disadvantage.

The player and their sibling arrive at the Unionist Training Camp with a number of other youths. The new conscripts are expected to Safari Zone-esque catch their starter from a small reserve, but the player and their sibling don’t need to, so they are sent to collect some items.
